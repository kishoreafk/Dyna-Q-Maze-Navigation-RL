Calibrate first, then compare ten methods

Before any comparison, the detector is calibrated on validation mazes (seeds 1004–1007, disjoint from training seeds 1000–1003): the CUSUM threshold h sets the false-alarm budget, and the repair gain κ sets how aggressively counters are re-armed after an alarm. Both are chosen from measured grids, not heuristics.
