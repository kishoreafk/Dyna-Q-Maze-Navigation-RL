CAVR-Dyna

Change-Aware Stochastic Model-Based RL for Dynamic Mazes

A Dyna-style tabular agent that learns a categorical Dirichlet transition model and a factored edge-belief map under action slip, detects topology changes with a calibrated CUSUM on the frozen pre-update prediction, and repairs by counterfactual prioritized backups under a value-of-computation budget. Hypothesis evaluated empirically — every number in this deck comes from actually executed runs.
