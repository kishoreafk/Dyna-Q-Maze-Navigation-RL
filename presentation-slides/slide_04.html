METHOD

Five mechanisms, one repair loop

01
Categorical Dirichlet model
per-(s,a) outcome counts give expected transitions and rewards under slip; a factored edge-belief map tracks per-edge log-odds of being open.
02
Calibrated CUSUM detector
two-level change detection on the FROZEN pre-update prediction; per-(s,a) alarms with false-alarm budget enforced by design.
03
Repair: local forgetting
on alarm, forget the affected model region (epoch bump) so stale experience stops poisoning planning.
04
Counterfactual prioritized backups
goal-conditioned expected value of each repair pushes backups through an epoch-versioned priority heap; stale entries invalidate automatically.
05
VoC budget + exploration floor
value-of-computation-style arbiter picks the backup budget B ∈ {0, 8, 16, 32, 64}; a nonzero ε-floor keeps hidden shortcuts rediscoverable.
